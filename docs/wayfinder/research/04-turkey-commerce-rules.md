## 1. KDV (VAT) rates for clothing, underwear, shoes, accessories

- General KDV rate is 20% and the reduced "(II) sayili liste" rate is 10%; the "(I) sayili liste" rate stays 1%. Presidential Decision No. 7346, Resmi Gazete 7 July 2023, issue 32241, amends Decision 2007/13033: Madde 1 changes "%18" to "%20" (general rate) and "%8" to "%10" (rate for the (II) list). Effective on the third day after publication (10 July 2023). Source for the text: reprint at [alomaliye.com](https://www.alomaliye.com/2023/07/07/katma-deger-vergisi-oranlari-karar-sayisi-7346/); the official gazette PDF ([RG 7 July 2023](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2023/07/20230707-4.pdf) is the TBMM decision file; the 7346 file itself was not text-readable by my fetcher). Treat the reprint as secondary.
- The (II) list, section "B) Diger mal ve hizmetler", names (official-consolidated text of Decision 2007/13033, hosted by TURMOB, pre-2023 so it shows the old 8% rate): item 4 fabrics (mensucat); item 5 "ic ve dis giyim esyasi (sapka, kravat, kaskol, kemer, corap, eldiven vb. dahil)", towels, bathrobes, bedding and home textiles; item 6 processed leather and garments made of it; item 7 "Her nevi maddeden mamul ayakkabi, terlik, cizme ve benzerleri" (footwear of any material); item 8 "Her nevi maddeden mamul canta, bavul, valiz ve benzerleri" (bags, suitcases); item 10 contract textile and garment work. [TURMOB: KDV oranlari (2007/13033 consolidated)](https://www.turmob.org.tr/arsiv/mbs/pratikBilgiler/9_kdv_oranlari.pdf). I read only part of the list; the file is an old consolidation (created 2012).
- Net reading: clothing (including underwear, as "ic giyim"), footwear, bags, belts, hats, gloves, socks fall under the (II) list and therefore 10% after 7346. Secondary 2026 blog sources say the rates are still %1/%10/%20 and that textile was not changed in 2025-2026, but I found no primary source for 2024-2026 amendments either way. [evobulut 2026 sector rates](https://www.evobulut.com/blog/sektorel-kdv-oranlari-2026), [Aposkal 2026 list](https://aposkal.com/blog/kdv-oranlari-2026-guncel-liste/) (both secondary, not verified).

## 2. Price display (KDV-inclusive)

- Distance-sales regulation, Madde 5(1)(d): the pre-contract information must include "Mal veya hizmetin tum vergiler dahil toplam fiyati", plus all shipping, delivery and similar extra costs, or the fact that extra costs may be charged if they cannot be pre-calculated. If the seller fails to disclose extra costs, the consumer does not have to pay them (Madde 5(3)). Official text read. [Mesafeli Sozlesmeler Yonetmeligi](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/yonetmelik/7.5.20237.pdf)
- Price tag regulation (Fiyat Etiketi Yonetmeligi): labels and price lists must show "Malin tum vergiler dahil satis fiyati" (Madde 5(2)(c)); "satis fiyati" is defined as the cash price including all taxes (Madde 4(1)(h)); if the shelf and till prices differ, the consumer-favourable price applies (Madde 10(1)). Labels and lists must be in Turkish. Official text read. [Fiyat Etiketi Yonetmeligi](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/yonetmelik/7.5.19819.pdf). The regulation text does not mention websites explicitly; Ticaret Bakanligi guidance extends price-information rules to ads and discounted sales on websites. [Ticaret Bakanligi price-advertising guide](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/data/6258153813b876385c84e95c/F%C4%B0YAT%20B%C4%B0LG%C4%B0S%C4%B0%20%C4%B0%C3%87EREN%20REKLAMLAR%20%C4%B0LE%20%C4%B0ND%C4%B0R%C4%B0ML%C4%B0%20SATI%C5%9E%20REKLAMLARI%20VE%20T%C4%B0CAR%C4%B0%20UYGULAMALARI%20HAKKINDA%20KILAVUZ.pdf) (read via search summary only).
- Discount display: the discounted price and the pre-discount price must both be shown. The pre-discount price is the lowest price applied in the 10 days before the discount began (Madde 11(1), as amended by RG 11 Oct 2025, issue 33044; an earlier version used 30 days per a search summary). Burden of proof is on the seller. Official text read. [Fiyat Etiketi Yonetmeligi](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/yonetmelik/7.5.19819.pdf)
- Mesafeli Madde 19: an extra charge beyond the agreed price needs the consumer's separate explicit consent, and pre-ticked options that cause extra payment must be refunded immediately. Relevant to a COD fee or paid gift wrap. Official text read. [Mesafeli Sozlesmeler Yonetmeligi](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/yonetmelik/7.5.20237.pdf)

## 3. Distance selling (mesafeli satis) obligations

Legal basis: Law 6502 Madde 48 and the Mesafeli Sozlesmeler Yonetmeligi (RG 27 Nov 2014, no. 29188), as amended through RG 24 May 2025 (no. 32909). Official texts read. [Law 6502](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6502.pdf), [Yonetmelik](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/yonetmelik/7.5.20237.pdf)

**Pre-contract information (Yonetmelik Madde 5-8; Law Madde 48(2))**
- Before the consumer accepts the contract or an offer, the seller must give the consumer all of these (Madde 5(1)): (a) essential characteristics of the goods; (b) seller's name or title and MERSIS number or tax ID; (c) full address, phone and similar contact details; (c-cedilla) complaint contact details if different; (d) total price including all taxes and any delivery/extra costs; (f) payment, delivery time and other delivery information, complaint-resolution methods; (g) the conditions, period and procedure for withdrawal and information on the seller's designated return carrier; (g-breve) the address, fax or e-mail for the withdrawal notice; (h) where withdrawal is excluded under Madde 15, that the consumer cannot withdraw or under what conditions the right is lost; (k) that consumers may apply to the consumer arbitration committee (tuketici hakem heyeti) or, with mandatory prior mediation (Law Madde 73/A), the consumer court.
- Presentation (Madde 6): at least 12 pt, clear and readable, in writing or on a durable data carrier. For internet sales the seller must, immediately before the consumer takes on the payment obligation, show again separately and clearly the items in (a), (d), (g) and (h) as a whole (Madde 6(2)(a)), and must state shipping restrictions and accepted payment methods at the latest before the order is placed (Madde 6(2)(b)).
- Confirmation (Madde 7): the seller must have the consumer confirm, in a way suited to the medium, that they obtained the information; otherwise "sozlesme kurulmamis sayilir" (the contract is treated as not formed).
- The Law (Madde 48(2)) says the consumer must be told clearly that placing the order creates a payment obligation. The Turkish text of Madde 48(2) puts the burden of proving the information was given on the seller. The Yonetmelik confirms this in Madde 5(6).
- The pre-information is an inseparable part of the distance contract and cannot be changed unless parties expressly agree (Madde 5(2)).
- The seller must keep information and documents on withdrawal, information duties and delivery for each transaction for 3 years (Madde 20(1)).

**Delivery (Law Madde 48(3); Yonetmelik Madde 16-17)**
- Seller must perform within the promised time; for goods this may never exceed 30 days (except custom-made goods). If it fails, the consumer may terminate and must be refunded within 14 days of the termination notice, with statutory interest.
- If delivery becomes impossible, the seller must notify the consumer in writing/durable medium within 3 days and refund within 14 days. "Malin stokta bulunmamasi" (out of stock) is explicitly NOT treated as impossibility (Madde 16(4)). This matters for stock shared with a physical shop.
- The seller bears loss and damage until the goods reach the consumer or a person the consumer names (not the carrier's choice of the consumer) (Madde 17).

**Withdrawal (cayma hakki) (Law Madde 48(4); Yonetmelik Madde 9-15)**
- 14 days, no reason and no penalty (Madde 9(1)). For goods, the period starts when the consumer (or a person the consumer names) receives the goods, not when the seller hands them to a carrier (Madde 9(2), 9(4)). The consumer may also withdraw between order and delivery.
- If the seller did not properly inform the consumer of the withdrawal right, the 14-day period does not bind the consumer; the right then lasts at most one year after the normal period would have ended (Law 48(4), Madde 10).
- The withdrawal notice may be written or on a durable data carrier; it only needs to be sent within the 14 days. The consumer may use the form in the regulation's annex or any clear statement. If the seller offers a form on its website it must send back confirmation immediately (Madde 11). Burden of proof of using the right is on the consumer.
- The consumer must return the goods within 14 days of notifying withdrawal unless the seller offers to collect them (Madde 13(1)). The consumer is not liable for changes or deterioration from normal use within the period (Law 48(4), Madde 13(2)).
- Refund (Madde 12, wording from the 2022 amendment, applicable since 1 Jan 2026 per Gecici Madde 1): the seller must refund all payments, including the original delivery cost, within 14 days of the goods being handed to the return carrier named in the pre-information (or of the goods reaching the seller if another carrier is used). The refund must be made in one go and without cost to the consumer. For card payments the card issuer credits the limit. Before delivery, the 14 days run from the notice.
- Return shipping cost (Madde 12(5), as replaced by RG 24 May 2025): if the consumer returns goods through the carrier the seller named for returns, the consumer cannot be charged return costs. If the seller named no carrier in the pre-information, the consumer cannot be charged any return cost either. If the named carrier has no branch where the consumer lives, the seller must collect the goods without extra cost.
- Transition: Gecici Madde 1 delayed Madde 5(1)(g), Madde 12, Madde 12/A(7)(b) and parts of Madde 13 and 15 until 1 Jan 2026; today (Nov 2026) the new versions apply. Official text read.

**Exceptions that matter for clothing (Madde 15(1))**
- (b) goods made to the consumer's specification or personal needs; (c) perishables; (c-cedilla) "Tesliminden sonra ambalaj, bant, muhur, paket gibi koruyucu unsurlari acilmis olan mallardan; iadesi saglik ve hijyen acisindan uygun olmayanlarin teslimine iliskin sozlesmeler" (goods whose protective packaging was opened after delivery and which cannot be returned for health or hygiene reasons). The regulation text does not name underwear. Ticaret Bakanligi consumer guidance gives underwear and earrings as examples that lose the right once the packaging is opened (search summary of the guide: [tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr guide](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/yayinlar/tuketici-bilgi-rehberi/mesafeli-sozlesmeler-hakkinda-bilgilendirme)); one passage in that summary read as contradictory, so check the guide text. **[LAWYER]** should word the underwear/swimwear/hosiery policy and the packaging-seal wording, since the exception applies only if packaging was opened AND the item is hygienically unsuitable to return.
- Danistay 10th Chamber annulled Madde 15(1)(i-dotted-less), (j), (k) (vehicles, live auctions, installed goods) on 6 May 2026 (noted in the consolidated text); not relevant to clothing.

## 4. KVKK (personal data) basics for guest checkout

Law 6698, official text read (version includes the 2024 amendments by Law 7499). [KVKK Law 6698](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6698.pdf)
- Madde 5(1): personal data cannot be processed without explicit consent (acik riza), but Madde 5(2) lists conditions that do not need consent, including (c) processing that is necessary for forming or performing a contract with the data subject, (c-cedilla) the controller's legal obligation (e.g. invoicing), (e) establishing, using or protecting a right, and (f) legitimate interests that do not harm the data subject.
- Therefore the order/delivery/invoice data of a guest buyer fall under the contract and legal-obligation conditions in Madde 5(2) by their text; explicit consent is not the condition named for those. NOT CONFIRMED in a KVKK Kurumu text which exact fields the Authority treats as "necessary"; **[LAWYER]** should map each checkout field to a lawful basis.
- Madde 10: at the time data is collected the controller must inform the data subject of: controller identity, purposes, to whom and why data may be transferred, the method and legal basis of collection, and the Madde 11 rights. This duty applies regardless of whether consent is needed.
- Aydinlatma Tebligi (RG 10 Mar 2018, no. 30356) and the KVKK guide: the notice and any explicit consent must be done separately; do not bundle them in one text or one checkbox. Board decision 2018/90 found a single checkbox for reading the notice and consenting, with mandatory registration, non-compliant; Board decision 2019/206 found a website privacy policy that did not separate notice and consent non-compliant and treated processing as a condition of service. These two points come from search results and KVKK summaries, not from the full decision text. [Aydinlatma Tebligi (RG)](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2018/03/20180310-5.htm), [KVKK 2018/90](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/5420/2018-90), [KVKK 2019/206](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6709/2019-206), [KVKK Aydinlatma guide](https://kvkk.gov.tr/SharedFolderServer/CMSFiles/a569a068-c079-4189-b134-f57bc727af7d.pdf)
- Madde 12: technical and organisational security measures are mandatory; breaches must be reported to the data subject and the Board "en kisa surede" (as soon as possible) (Madde 12(5)). Madde 13: data-subject requests must be answered within 30 days, free of charge.
- Madde 9 (as amended 2024): transfer abroad needs an adequacy decision, or one of the listed safeguards, or other listed conditions. Relevant if the VPS, email provider or analytics sit outside Turkey. I read only the first part of Madde 9; the full list of safeguards and the notification duties were not read. **[LAWYER]**.
- VERBIS (data controller registry, Madde 16): registration is required unless exempt. Current exemption: fewer than 50 employees AND annual balance-sheet total below TRY 100 million, and main activity is not special-category data (Decision 2018/87 as amended; 2023/1154 raised the balance-sheet limit). Decision 2025/2393 (25 Dec 2025) clarifies that controllers that do not keep balance-sheet books are judged on employee count alone. Exemption from registration does not exempt a controller from the rest of the Law. [KVKK announcement on Decision 2025/1572 and 2025/2393](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8577/kisisel-verileri-koruma-kurulunun-04-09-2025-tarihli-ve-2025-1572-sayili-kararinin-uygulama-esaslarina-iliskin-kamuoyu-duyurusu) (read via fetcher summary), [Sorularla VERBIS](https://verbis.kvkk.gov.tr/sharedFolder/sorularla-verbis.pdf?ts=20251006) (listed, not read).
- Marketing messages are separate: Law 6563 Madde 6(1) requires prior consent for commercial electronic messages (email/SMS); no separate consent is needed for messages about change, use or maintenance of goods or services bought where the contact info was given for contact. Madde 6(2): messages to merchants/tradespeople (esnaf, tacir) don't need prior consent. Official text read. [Law 6563](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6563.pdf). The Iletisim Yonetim Sistemi (IYS) registry was not researched here; NOT CONFIRMED whether IYS registration applies to this seller.
- Guest-checkout specifics: NOT CONFIRMED that any rule treats guest checkout differently from account checkout. The Madde 10 notice applies to both.

## 5. Cash on delivery and bank transfer

- Law 6502 / Mesafeli Yonetmelik contain no prohibition or special regime for cash on delivery (kapida odeme) or for bank transfer (havale/EFT); I found no primary text that mentions either. The Yonetmelik only requires the seller to state, at the latest before the order is placed, which payment methods are accepted and whether any shipping restriction applies (Madde 6(2)(b)). Official text read. [Mesafeli Sozlesmeler Yonetmeligi](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/yonetmelik/7.5.20237.pdf)
- Ticaret Bakanligi has warned consumers that payment methods lacking account-owner information, such as cash on delivery on non-corporate sites and social media, can cause harm and advises reading the pre-information first (search summary of [Ticaret Bakanligi statement](https://ticaret.gov.tr/haberler/mesafeli-satislara-iliskin-basin-aciklamasi)). This is guidance for consumers, not a rule on sellers.
- Extra fees: a COD fee or similar extra charge needs the consumer's separate explicit consent and must be disclosed in the total price/extra costs (Mesafeli Madde 5(1)(d), Madde 19). Official text read.
- Refunds: Madde 12(4) requires refunds in the same payment instrument the consumer used (the text of the 24 May 2025 amendment removed a phrase from the previous sentence; the current text reads "tuketicinin satin alirken kullandigi odeme aracina uygun bir sekilde"), in one payment, with no cost to the consumer. Applying this to a COD or bank-transfer sale (refund to an IBAN) is my reading; the text does not discuss it. **[LAWYER]**.
- Who is allowed to collect cash for the seller (carrier collection / tahsilatli gonderi) and the tax treatment of money held by the carrier were NOT researched. The seller's invoice still has to be issued (section 6). **[ACCOUNTANT]**.
- NOT CONFIRMED: when exactly the 30-day delivery period starts for a bank-transfer order that is paid later; Madde 16(1) says it runs from the date the order reaches the seller. **[LAWYER]**.
- Whether a "yazar kasa" (ODK, cash register) receipt applies to online or COD sales was NOT researched. GIB treats e-Arsiv as the document for internet sales (section 6).

## 6. e-Fatura / e-Arsiv obligation

Primary source: Vergi Usul Kanunu Genel Tebligi Sira No 509 (GIB consolidated version incorporating Tebligs 515, 526, 535, 550, 573, and a 2025 amendment 589). I extracted the GIB consolidated PDF locally; its page footnote cites changes up to Teblig 589 (in force 31 Dec 2025). [GIB: VUK 509 consolidated, 2026](https://cdn.gib.gov.tr/api/gibportal-file/file/getFile?objectKey=MEVZUAT_TEBLIGLER%2FUNIVERSAL%2F2026%2FMEVZUAT_TEBLIGLER_2026_VukTeb509_Guncel.pdf)

- IV.1.4(a)(4): sellers of goods or services on their own internet site or other electronic media must join e-Fatura if their gross sales revenue is 500,000 TL or more (the limit for accounting periods 2022 and later). IV.1.5(c): they must apply and be ready by the start of the 7th month of the year after the period in which the limit was reached. Sellers below the limit may join voluntarily (IV.1.4(d), as read in the general text).
- IV.2.4.2 mirrors this for e-Arsiv: such sellers must switch to e-Arsiv Fatura (or e-Fatura) by the same dates; from then on their invoices must be e-Fatura or e-Arsiv Fatura (except for the cases in sections V.7 and VIII).
- IV.2.4.3 (amended by Tebligs 573 and 589): taxpayers not in the e-Arsiv system must issue e-Arsiv Fatura through the GIB portal or a licensed integrator for invoices over 3,000 TL (tax-inclusive) in 2025; from 1 Jan 2026 regardless of amount. For taxpayers on the basit usul (simplified) tax regime and for those keeping books on the isletme hesabi basis the amount-based rule continues until 31 Dec 2026, and the all-amounts rule starts 1 Jan 2027. A paper (matbu) invoice where e-Arsiv is required triggers the penalty in VUK Madde 353 for each invoice (IV.2.4.3 and IV.2.5).
- IV.2.4.5 (internet sales via e-Arsiv): e-Arsiv invoices for internet sales must be delivered electronically and must show the web address of the sale, the payment method, the payment date, the carrier's name and tax ID (for goods), the dispatch date, the return section, and the sentence "Bu satis internet uzerinden yapilmistir." A copy of the invoice printout, ÖKC receipt or waybill must travel with the goods.
- IV.2.4.4: GIB may order any taxpayer judged risky to move to e-Arsiv with at least 3 months' notice.
- Reading for this project, which depends on facts I do not have: because the store's online sales are the type in IV.2.4.2, and the invoice rules in IV.2.4.3 now apply to every non-e-Arsiv taxpayer from 1 Jan 2026 (or 1 Jan 2027 for basit usul / isletme hesabi), an online store seller will in practice need to issue e-Arsiv Fatura for each online sale, whether or not it reaches the 500,000 TL limit. Which of these clauses applies depends on the seller's tax regime and how the shop and the website are registered. **[ACCOUNTANT]** must confirm. NOT CONFIRMED: whether a sole proprietor on basit usul is within the e-Arsiv obligation for online sales before 2027.
- I did not read the e-Arsiv Fatura technical guide on ebelge.gib.gov.tr (the integrator vs portal options, invoice data schema, e-Arsiv report submission, and the ability to issue to buyers without a tax ID). Those matter for the integration ticket. [GIB e-Belge site](https://ebelge.gib.gov.tr) (not read).
