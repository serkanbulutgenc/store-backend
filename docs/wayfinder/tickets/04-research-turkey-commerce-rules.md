## Question

Establish facts from primary sources: current KDV rates applicable to clothing/underwear/shoes/accessories; price-display requirements (KDV-inclusive); mesafeli satis (distance selling) obligations, cayma hakki/return window and required pre-checkout info; KVKK consent basics for guest checkout; cash-on-delivery and bank-transfer (havale/EFT) practices; whether e-Arsiv/e-Fatura is mandatory for this seller size. Flag anything needing an accountant/lawyer. Write findings to research/04-turkey-commerce-rules.md with source links.

## Resolution

Findings: [research/04-turkey-commerce-rules.md](../research/04-turkey-commerce-rules.md)

- KDV: clothing, underwear, footwear, bags, belts/hats/gloves sit on the (II) list at 10% (Decision 7346, 2023); general rate 20%; accessories such as jewellery are not shown to be on the 10% list (accountant to classify). Prices must be shown tax-inclusive.
- Distance selling: mandatory pre-contract info and a re-display of key items right before payment, a confirmation step (else no contract), 14-day withdrawal, 30-day max delivery, seller-paid return via named carrier, refund in 14 days; opened hygiene goods (underwear) excepted. Out of stock is not an excuse for non-delivery.
- KVKK: contract and legal-obligation bases cover order data; aydinlatma notice is separate from explicit consent; marketing messages need separate consent. VERBIS exempt below 50 employees and 100M TL balance sheet. COD and havale have no special statute; extra fees need explicit consent.
- e-Arsiv: online sellers at 500k TL or more must use e-Fatura/e-Arsiv; non-registered taxpayers must issue e-Arsiv for all amounts from 1 Jan 2026 (1 Jan 2027 for basit usul / isletme hesabi). Needs accountant confirmation, as do the legal wording items flagged in the file.
