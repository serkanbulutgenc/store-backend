# Staff are trusted with correctable actions; only Destructive actions are Owner-only

The Owner/Staff split is drawn at **Destructive actions** (money going out, or removing something for good) and **Revenue data** (aggregates and margin), not at "anything risky". Staff may take any action whose mistakes can be corrected by editing, such as stock adjustments, price edits and photo changes. The Audit log is the safety net for those.

We rejected bounded Staff limits (for example a cap on one stock adjustment) and Owner approval for sensitive Staff actions. With 1-5 Dashboard users, both slow the everyday work more than they prevent mistakes. Anyone later tempted to add limits or approvals should first check whether the mistake they fear can be corrected by editing and is in the Audit log.

**Known exception: marking an Order paid.** Staff may do it, because a bank transfer cannot be processed otherwise, but Staff cannot undo it: the Order moves to preparing and the Shopper is emailed. It is guarded instead (only from unpaid, amount must equal the Order total, audit-logged). A wrong one is fixed by the Owner cancelling the paid Order and recording a refund.
