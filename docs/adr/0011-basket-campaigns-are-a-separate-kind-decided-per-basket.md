# A Basket Campaign is a separate kind of rule, decided per basket and frozen on the Order as a discount

A Basket Campaign (minimum basket amount, then a fixed TL amount off, a percentage off, or free shipping) is stored as its own rule, apart from the Campaign that prices items. It is looked up when a basket is quoted, never written onto Products. The quote finds the best qualifying one on the items at Campaign prices, spreads its discount over the item lines the way a Voucher's is spread, and a Voucher then applies on top. The Order freezes the lines at what the Shopper paid, records the Basket Campaign's name, rule and amount, and links to it for reporting.

We rejected adding a threshold and a fixed amount to the existing Campaign. A Campaign is a price: it is computed per Product on every read (ADR 0009), shown struck through on cards and the product page, and sorted and filtered on. A basket threshold has no meaning for a card, and a fixed amount per item has awkward edges (an item cheaper than the amount). Mixing the two would also make every price surface understand a basket. We also rejected modelling it as an automatic Voucher: a Voucher has a code, a redemption count and a once-per-Customer rule that an automatic offer does not need, and its Oscar models would carry a code-less variant of all of it.

Because the discount is spread over the lines like a Voucher's, everything that reads line prices (the Order total, refunds that return what the Shopper paid, reports) stays correct without knowing a Basket Campaign exists. Free shipping is the same rule with a different benefit: it competes with the others on what it is worth to the Shopper (the shipping fee that would be charged), so only one benefit applies per basket, and a Basket Campaign for a discount and one for free shipping do not combine.

Consequences: the quote needs the shipping fee before it chooses, so shipping is worked out first. A Basket Campaign cannot be priced at read time for a listing, so the storefront learns of it only in the basket and at checkout, and a basket nudge ("add X more") would be a separate, later feature. Tiers are several Basket Campaigns, one threshold each. If the Owner later wants a discount and free shipping together, that is a decision to change "only the best one applies", not a change to how the discount is stored.
